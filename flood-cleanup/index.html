Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Logan Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Logan to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Logan

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.
