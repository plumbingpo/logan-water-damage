How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
